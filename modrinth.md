## Description 

</center>

**Pride** _(or Warrior's Pride)_ is a mod that aim to revert Minecraft PvP physics to pre 1.9, while still keeping the content of the Combat Update.

Pride reworks the **knockback** functions, and **attack cooldowns** to make swords and fishing rods from 1.8.9 / 1.7.10 integrate with the new axes and shields. It also tries to replicate the feel of old school PvP by changing the player's **body rotation speed**, and the buckets / sword **animations**. 

<br>
<br>

## Feature List : 

* Knockback based on 1.8 mappings
* Damage calculation based on 1.8 mappings
* Fishing Rod physics based on 1.8 mappings
* Body / head rotation based on 1.8 mappings
* Working Hit and Block system (When no shield equipped) with configurable damage reduction
* Attack cooldown disabled for items, but kept for axes
* Nerfed Axe damage
* Disabled Crit, Weak and Strong hit sounds
* Disabled Sweeping attacks

All of these functionalities can be enabled / disabled via **Pride**'s config file !
